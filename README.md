# Team Playbooks

Workflows and templates for Oleena's team of leads (Chief of Staff, Marketing, Creative, Engineer, Product, and GTM Leads).

## Start a campaign

Read [agent instructions](AGENTS.md), then follow [brief creation](playbooks/campaign-brief-creation.md). Start with a voice dump; Chief of Staff helps clarify it before research and final Gate 1 approval. See [setup steps](setup/campaign-brief.md) and the [skill catalog](skills/README.md). Existing production and launch gates remain in place.

## Playbooks

- [`playbooks/CONVENTIONS.md`](playbooks/CONVENTIONS.md): rules every lead skill and playbook follows — hard rules, gate order, routing, defaults, skill format, and standard output contract.
- [`playbooks/campaign-brief-creation.md`](playbooks/campaign-brief-creation.md): active workflow from voice intake through an approved campaign brief. Production workflows will be defined separately.
- [`playbooks/landing-pages.md`](playbooks/landing-pages.md): landing page conventions for gates 3-4 — section architecture, copy as source of truth, the 5-second phone test, and pre-ship checklist.

## Templates

- [`templates/campaign-brief.md`](templates/campaign-brief.md): the brief to fill in at the start of each campaign.
- [`templates/campaign-intent.md`](templates/campaign-intent.md): clarified intent from the initial conversation.
- [`templates/campaign-research-plan.md`](templates/campaign-research-plan.md): research approach, time, cost and scope approval.
- [`templates/campaign-record.md`](templates/campaign-record.md): campaign state, decisions and version-specific approvals.
- [`templates/taste-loop.md`](templates/taste-loop.md): turn Oleena's corrections into gold examples and yes/no checks to run before sending work.

Changes go through pull requests. Nothing merges without Oleena's approval.
