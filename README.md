# Team Playbooks

Workflows and templates for Oleena's team of leads (Chief of Staff, Marketing, Creative, Engineer, Product, and GTM Leads).

- [`playbooks/campaign-playbook.md`](playbooks/campaign-playbook.md): how a campaign moves from idea to approved launch to measured results, with roles and approval gates.
- [`templates/campaign-brief.md`](templates/campaign-brief.md): the brief to fill in at the start of each campaign.

Changes go through pull requests. Nothing merges without Oleena's approval.
