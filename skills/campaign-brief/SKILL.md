# Campaign Brief

Read ../../playbooks/CONVENTIONS.md and ../../playbooks/campaign-brief-creation.md. Load campaign intent, research summary, relevant findings, decisions and prototype feedback.

1. Present a recommended direction, alternatives and tradeoffs. Ask Oleena to resolve material commercial choices; do not silently select an offer.
2. For discovery, recommend the smallest useful experiment and identify what it can and cannot validate. Keep broader initiative ambitions separate from the campaign hypothesis.
3. Draft brief.md from ../../templates/campaign-brief.md. Use unknown where evidence is absent; agree defensible targets rather than invent benchmarks.
4. Add problem/solution/campaign hypotheses, intended fulfillment, audience exclusions, supported claims, dependencies and decision rules, including insufficient evidence.
5. Run the review checklist in the brief-creation playbook. Revise defects within scope; surface genuine choices. If new research is required, return for scoped approval.
6. Present one Gate 1 packet: result, files/version, evidence/assumptions, recommendation, unresolved decisions, next step and requested approval.
7. Chief of Staff records approval against the exact version in campaign-record.md. Material audience/offer/goal/budget/test changes reopen Gate 1. Stop before production; existing per-run and launch approvals still apply.

Completion: reviewed brief and an explicit recorded decision. Draft complete is not approved.
