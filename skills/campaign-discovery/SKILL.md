# Campaign Discovery

Read ../../playbooks/CONVENTIONS.md and ../../playbooks/campaign-brief-creation.md. Resolve these paths relative to this skill folder. Identify the campaign and load existing intake, decisions and relevant context before asking questions.

1. Preserve the raw dump in intake.md. Ask whether an ambiguous phrase changes intent before interpreting it.
2. Distinguish the broader initiative from the specific decision/campaign. Ask a few high-impact questions at a time; recommend options rather than merely echoing the user.
3. Challenge assumptions and feasibility. Do not assume execution is trivial or bigger bets are always better.
4. If uncertainty is best resolved by reaction, make or propose a minimal offer card, sample output, outline or mock. Timebox it; respect existing heavy-production approval rules. Save in prototypes/ and record reaction as preference evidence, not market validation.
5. Draft intent.md using ../../templates/campaign-intent.md. Unknowns are valid. Confirm goal, motivation, hypotheses, commitments, constraints and research questions with Oleena.
6. Record confirmation for the exact version in campaign-record.md. Hand the confirmed intent to research scoping. Do not conduct expanded research, finalize an offer or begin production implicitly.

Completion: confirmed intent, recorded decisions and a bounded set of research questions. If the user redirects, preserve the campaign state and follow their scope.
