# Campaign Research

Read ../../playbooks/CONVENTIONS.md and ../../playbooks/campaign-brief-creation.md. Load intent.md and relevant decisions; resolve repository references relative to this skill folder.

1. Draft research/plan.md from ../../templates/campaign-research-plan.md. Name the decisions supported, methods, relevant sources, exclusions, freshness, output paths, active/elapsed estimates and model/API/tool limits.
2. Obtain Oleena's approval of that concrete scope. Do not invent approved ceilings. Respect existing per-run worker approval requirements.
3. Inspect relevant internal documentation and prior results. Link canonical sources; record unavailable access and stale information.
4. Research external evidence according to the approved questions. Search/page reading are sufficient initially; do not add tools or subscriptions without a concrete need and authorization.
5. Write research/findings.md: question, observation, source/path, date checked, inference, confidence and limitations. Distinguish observed competitor activity from performance evidence.
6. Write research/summary.md: answers, contradictions, implications, options/recommendation and remaining gaps. Return compact findings with file links and actual usage where available.
7. Stop when questions are addressed or limits reached. On blockers or scope expansion, return partial evidence and a precise next decision; do not quietly retry or expand.

Write only assigned research files. Return to Chief of Staff for decisions; do not treat research conclusions as approved positioning or update shared business truths automatically.
